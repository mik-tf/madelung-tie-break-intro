# Who goes first when two levels tie?

Author: Michaël Perreault

An introduction, in plain language, to the research paper *A first-order Madelung tie-break on the round four-sphere: Kelvin parity, convex order and the Thomas-Fermi field* (concept DOI [10.5281/zenodo.24533673](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24533673)). The paper has the proofs. This note has the idea, and it says what the paper does and does not claim.
